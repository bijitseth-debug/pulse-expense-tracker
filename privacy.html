PULSE Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PULSE, a personal expense tracking application, handles information when you use it.

1. Information stored by PULSE

PULSE allows you to enter financial information such as income, expenses, transaction descriptions, dates, categories and monthly budget amounts.

This information is stored locally in your browser using LocalStorage on your device. PULSE does not require an account to use its core features.

2. Data transmission

PULSE does not intentionally transmit transaction or budget information to external servers. Financial records are stored locally on your device.

The application uses its service worker to retrieve and cache application files from the same website so that it can work offline. This caching does not intentionally include your financial records.

3. Hosting and technical information

PULSE is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub may process technical information associated with website requests, such as IP addresses and browser or device information, under its own policies.

This hosting-related processing is separate from the financial records stored by PULSE.

4. Backups and imports

PULSE provides backup export and import features. Exported backup files may contain your financial records and budget information.

Backup files are handled through your device and browser. You are responsible for keeping exported backups secure and deciding where to store or share them.

5. Data security and limitations

PULSE uses browser LocalStorage, which is not encrypted by the application. Anyone who gains sufficient access to your device, browser profile or application environment may be able to access locally stored information.

No application can guarantee complete security. Keep your device protected and avoid sharing backup files containing personal financial information.

6. Data deletion and control

You can manage or delete transactions using the application's available features. You can also remove PULSE's locally stored website data through your browser settings.

Removing browser site data may delete your transactions, budget and other locally stored information. Export a backup first if you want to retain your records.

You are also responsible for deleting exported backup files from your device and any other location where you have saved them.

7. Third-party services

PULSE's core expense-tracking functionality does not require third-party analytics or advertising services. External hosting and services may operate under their own privacy policies.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if PULSE's features, technology or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or PULSE, you can contact the application maintainer through the PULSE GitHub repository .
